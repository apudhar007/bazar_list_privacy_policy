Section 01Overview

Welcome to Bazar List — your smart daily market companion, built by Apdroid System. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the Bazar List application on Android.

💡 We built Bazar List with a privacy-first philosophy. No account. No login. No personal data collected by us. Your shopping data lives entirely on your device.

By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy. Please read it carefully.

🗄️

Section 02Data We (Don't) Collect

✅ Apdroid System does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. We do not require your name, email address, phone number, location, or any identifying information.

The only data created within the app is your shopping data — item names, quantities, prices, and dates — and this data never leaves your device.

  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No personal identification information collected
  • No shopping data sent to our servers (we have none)
  • No analytics or crash reporting that identifies you personally
📦

Section 03Local Data Storage

Bazar List uses Android's Room Database — a local SQLite-based storage system — to save all your shopping records directly on your device. This enables the app to work fully offline.

📱 All data including shopping lists, item prices, quantities, history records, and expense summaries are stored locally on your device only.

The following types of data are stored locally:

  • Shopping list items (name, quantity, unit price, total)
  • Daily shopping records with date stamps
  • Historical shopping archive for past dates
  • Expense summary data (weekly, monthly, all-time totals)

This data is only accessible by the Bazar List app on your device and is never synchronized to any cloud service by us.

📢

Section 04Advertising — Google AdMob

To keep Bazar List free for everyone, we use Google AdMob (Google's Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements within the app. The following ad formats are used:

  • Adaptive Banner Ads — displayed at the bottom of screens
  • Native Ads — integrated into the app's UI (e.g., the Expense Summary screen) to blend naturally with the design
⚠️ Important: Google AdMob is a third-party service. To serve relevant ads and measure performance, Google may independently collect certain device-specific information.

Data that Google AdMob may collect includes (but is not limited to):

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Approximate or precise location data (based on your device settings)
  • Device type, operating system version, and hardware specs
  • App usage and interaction data
  • IP address and network information

This data collection is governed entirely by Google's own Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review it:

👉 Google Privacy Policy — policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized ads by adjusting your device's advertising settings under Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Section 05App Permissions

Bazar List requests the following Android permissions and uses them only for their stated purpose:

🌐 INTERNET ⚡ HARDWARE ACCELERATION 💾 STORAGE (Internal)
  • Internet Access: Required solely to load and display advertisements from Google AdMob. The app itself does not send your shopping data over the internet.
  • Hardware Acceleration: Enabled to ensure smooth UI performance, animations, and proper rendering of ad content.
  • Internal Storage: Used by the Room Database to persist your shopping lists and history on your device's private storage area.
🛡️

Section 06Your Data Control

You have complete control over your data in Bazar List. Since everything is stored locally on your device, you can manage it at any time:

  • Delete individual records: Remove any shopping entry or history item directly from the app interface.
  • Clear all data: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Bazar List → Storage → Clear Data to permanently erase all app data from your device.
  • Uninstall the app: Removes all associated data entirely from your device.
🔒 There is no account to delete, no server to request data removal from, and no backup copies of your data held by us. You are fully in control.
👶

Section 07Children's Privacy

Bazar List is a general-purpose shopping list application not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Since we collect no personal data ourselves, there is no risk of us inadvertently storing children's information. However, as Google AdMob may collect device-level data, parents and guardians should be aware of this and review Google's policies if the app is used by children.
